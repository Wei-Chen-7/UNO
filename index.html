How to play

Match the top card by color, number, or symbol — or play a Wild. Can't (or don't want to) play? Tap the deck to draw one; if it's playable you may play it right away.

Skip skips the next player. Reverse flips direction (acts like Skip with 2 players). +2 makes the next player draw 2 and lose a turn. Wild picks the color. Wild +4 picks the color and the next player draws 4.

First to empty their hand wins the round and scores everyone else's cards (numbers = face value, actions = 20, wilds = 50). First to 500 wins the match.
