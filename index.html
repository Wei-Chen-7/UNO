How to play

Match the top card by color, number, or symbol — or play a Wild. Can't (or don't want to) play? Tap the deck to draw one; if it's playable you may play it right away.

Skip skips the next player. Reverse flips direction (acts like Skip with 2 players). +2 makes the next player draw 2 and lose a turn. Wild picks the color. Wild +4 picks the color and the next player draws 4.

UNO! When you play down to one card, tap the UNO! button before the next player acts — or a bot may catch you and you draw 2. Bots call it too, but they sometimes forget: when a bot's avatar glows gold, hit CATCH! to make them draw 2.

First to empty their hand wins the round and scores everyone else's cards (numbers = face value, actions = 20, wilds = 50). First to 500 wins the match. Single-round mode ends after one hand.

House rules in Settings: stack +2 on +2 (pass an incoming +2 along by adding your own) and draw until playable.
